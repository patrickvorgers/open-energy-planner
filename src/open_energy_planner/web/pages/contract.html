How saved rules affect your plans

Each dated rule replaces the whole rule until the next starts. Original plans retain their original rates. Saving creates an immutable revision and revalues affected costs. Changed terms retain the supplier contract start and bonus budget. Enter a new contract start when switching supplier.

Rates use EUR/kWh. Import is a cost and export is income. Both may be negative. Market is the spot price before contract additions. Formula examples are illustrative, not supplier quotations.

Import formula: 2026
(market + 0.09161) * 1.21 + 0.02

Dutch electricity tax is €0.09161/kWh excluding VAT for the first 10,000 kWh per year in 2026. Zonneplan's published purchase fee is €0.02/kWh including VAT. Apply 21.0% VAT to the wholesale price and energy tax. Add the already VAT-inclusive fee afterwards so it is not taxed twice. At a wholesale price of €0.10/kWh the displayed import rate is €0.25/kWh. Formula constants retain their full precision. Displayed calculated amounts use two decimals.

Export formula
market + 0.02

The base example adds Zonneplan's published €0.02/kWh to the spot price. At market €0.06/kWh, base export income is €0.08/kWh. Keep the conditional 10.0% Zonnebonus in the separate bonus rule. This marginal export example excludes annual netting and energy-tax refunds.

market is the spot price in EUR/kWh supplied by the normal price adapters. Fixed subscription fees are outside these per-kWh formulas. Enabling a bonus adds its payment separately. Do not include it again in the export formula.

Zonneplan Zonnebonus

Zonneplan describes a base export payment plus an extra 10.0% when the market price plus its addition is positive, during daylight, up to 7,500 kWh per calendar year. Check your signed terms. This example is not an exact supplier preset.

For one exported kWh at a market price of €0.18/kWh, base income is €0.20 and the bonus is €0.02: total income €0.22. At night or after the cap, only the €0.20 base remains. With 0.50 kWh of quota left, that export earns €0.01 bonus and €0.21 total.

This app counts all grid export towards the cap, including unpaid hours and battery export. Source exclusions are unsupported. This example cannot reproduce contract terms that exclude particular energy sources. Changing terms within the same contract does not reset used volume.

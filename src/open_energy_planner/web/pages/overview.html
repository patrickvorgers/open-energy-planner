How is this calculated?

Saving = electricity cost without battery − electricity cost with battery

Recorded grid and battery flows reconstruct the same measured home and solar without storage. Both sides use the applicable electricity prices and the bonus rates and eligibility windows. Where the volume cap is ignored, the estimated bonus can exceed the supplier’s payment. Older finalized results may use the capped calculation.

Battery wear, investment costs and changes in the value of stored energy are excluded. Charging costs count when energy is bought. Stored energy used later can therefore shift the apparent saving between periods.

Solar curtailment and the timing of appliances, heating and EV charging remain as measured. This measures the battery's cash contribution, including its operation, and does not isolate every effect of energy management.

Missing measurements or prices leave gaps. Both costs include only the same accepted time. Negative savings stay visible. Today becomes eligible after local midnight, and missing evidence is retried during the recovery window.

Finalized results from the previous calculation are retained unchanged. They are not mixed into the new measured cash comparison.

EV charging

Charging sessions

Home Assistant automations control the physical charge points. Vehicle recognition runs automatically using available car signals, allowed vehicles and planned expectation. Ambiguous cars wait for distinguishing evidence. Recognition always belongs to the current physical connection.

Charging requests

Recognized arrivals automatically use the vehicle defaults. You can edit a request or explicitly repeat it on selected weekdays. Request overrides leave those defaults unchanged.

Planning and charging losses

A connected, recognized car is eligible with a planned charging request and a measured or last-known SoC. A car with no known SoC, unknown identity or a measurement-only point cannot authorize charging. A shortfall is shown if the modeled charging controls cannot deliver the target before departure. Hard installation limits always apply.

The planner uses the vehicle's charging efficiency to estimate how much AC energy a charge needs. You can adjust the single-phase or three-phase efficiency stages in EV settings. Temperature and battery conditioning can change actual consumption.

Departure times follow the Home Assistant installation timezone. There is no separate timezone setting. V2G is not supported.

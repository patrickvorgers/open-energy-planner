Planning and charging losses

A connected, explicitly identified car is eligible automatically. Missing SoC, unknown identity or a measurement-only point never authorizes charging. A shortfall is shown if the modeled charging controls cannot deliver the target before departure. Hard installation limits always apply.

Estimated AC charging losses use a documented power-dependent planning curve. Rounded defaults are 20.0% at 2.3 kW, 15.0% at 4.1 kW and 10.0% at 11 kW and above. Delivered power is interpolated between these points. These are planning assumptions, not measured vehicle efficiency. Temperature and battery conditioning can change actual consumption.

Departure times follow the Home Assistant installation timezone. There is no OEP timezone setting. V2G is not supported.

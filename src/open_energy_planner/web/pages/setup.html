    Planning strategy

    Compare optimization goalsMinimize cost chooses the lowest expected economic cost. Minimize consumption first reduces grid import plus the grid energy needed to replace depleted starting battery stock. It then chooses the lowest cost among equivalent plans. Export does not offset import. Mandatory reserve recovery comes first for both strategies.
